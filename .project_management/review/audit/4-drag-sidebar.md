## 2. Fixes, ranked (stack cleanliness gained per TV cost)

1. Register folded branches' drops as targets (or mount them hidden) so `useOffers` lists every nested drop. Fixes a floor violation that nested sidebar plus drop-by-menu introduced. Cost to TV: none.
2. Type `Drop`/`Dragged` over the procedure's selection query (`Q`) so `drag.picked` needs no cast. Pure types, removes `as never` at TV lib/playlists.tsx:70 and playlists.tsx:117.
3. Neutralise the TrackVault nouns in the guide examples, JSDoc and showcase (rules.md, descriptors.ts, apps/showcase library.tsx, a-drag-is-data.md). Zero code, zero TV cost.
4. Optional: one shared `treeitem` row between `PlaceTree` and the Section tree row (internal dedupe).

## 3. Leave as is

- Drop on a place and nested places: the pattern belongs to any app with scopes (projects, labels, boards, favourites). Several plausible callers, the shape carries no TV noun, it reuses `treeRows`/`treeMove` and the act wiring. It is not TrackVault's.
- `reorder` with `index` plus `after/before`, the held optimistic order, `Picked` payload, label/line/edge scroll, the Alt-arrow and menu floor, Shift+Enter/Context Menu keys: all expressed as data props, tested in ui-core (drag.test.ts, route.test.ts), documented in a decision record.
- Fold persistence in localStorage: obeys the storage rule.
- Web-only drag with `PlaceSpec.children`/`drop` ignored on the phone: documented.
- `ui-core/drag.ts` holding web-only arithmetic: justified by DOM-free testing.

## Could not decide

- Whether `Space` activating a tree row's anchor (tree.tsx:73) is intended: WAI-ARIA tree uses Space for selection; here it navigates. Minor.
- Whether `useOffers`'s "drops of one label are one entry" dedupe (lib/drag.ts:397) can hide two different drops that share a label (two playlists named alike): it keys on the label, not the target.
